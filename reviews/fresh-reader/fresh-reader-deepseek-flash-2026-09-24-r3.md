### Whole-paper retell

The paper proposes that agent continuity has three progressively deeper layers — memory persistence, narrative identity, and **epistemic identity** (a self maintained as calibrated, provenance-bearing, self-correcting knowledge) — and argues that a system it calls **ENGRAM** (an Epistemic Node Graph for Retraction, Arbitration, and Memory) is the first substrate to realize the third.
