## end-of-paper

**End-of-paper block**

**Whole-paper retell (one paragraph).**
ENGRAM is a typed-claim graph — observations, derivations, axioms, cornerstones, lessons, goals, feeling-reports, person nodes — in which every claim carries provenance, confidence is *computed* from the declared reasoning type rather than asserted, and withdrawing a claim automatically marks everything derived from it, with a per-turn hook and a sleep-time sweep guaranteeing the marks are eventually faced. The paper argues this realizes a third, deepest layer of agent continuity it calls epistemic identity; supports that by convergence across philosophy, cognitive science and belief revision, with Kahl's 2026 Ontological Continuity Condition as the operational endpoint; claims as its own contribution that an ENGRAM agent *is* the unique, no-merging, ancestry-traceable history of its claims, which fixes a retrieval profile and thereby behaviour; and reports the bounded survey claim that none of eleven first-hand sources satisfies Condition 3 strictly while ENGRAM does, plus one five-month, n = 1 graph (10,361 nodes, 9,399 current) showing a differentiated confidence distribution, a connected-but-evidence-carried structure, and two real retraction cascades. The average reader also takes away something rarer than the architecture: this is a paper that spends an unusual proportion of its length declaring what it has *not* shown — "the instruments are reproducible, the data they read is not yet open" — and the disclosure is part of the contribution's substance, not a compliance paragraph.

**Ordering: what I would move, and what it would fix.**

1. **Appendix E §E.2–§E.4 forward, into Chapter 4.** The node taxonomy, the edge-flag table, and the three-propagation-formula model are the system's actual specification, and they arrive last. Chapters 4, 5, 6 and Appendix B all defer to them ("Appendix E §E.3", "the full argument is §4.3's", "→ Appx C"), so a reader spends six chapters reading mechanisms described only by their effects. Most concretely: §6.1's evidence-type means cannot be checked against E.4's `quote_type` anchors without E.4, and the two do not reconcile (`attributed_analysis` 0.70 anchor against a 0.74 mean, with §6.1's mixture explanation covering only the two *lowest* means).
2. **Appendix B's summary table forward, into §4.4.** The paper's strongest claim — Condition 3 "strict; sole satisfier" — is asserted in §1.4 with no evidence, restated in §4.4 in one line, and evidenced only two appendices later. §4.4 already says "this chapter makes the synthesizing claim and points there"; inverting that would put eleven verdicts where the claim is made.
3. **Appendix D.4 into the body (Chapter 6 or 7).** The warm-briefing reversal — the film frame "did not survive", the date of the replacement, "No live warm-briefing has addressed its reader as Lucy since 2026-04-16" — is the only place the paper shows a design that failed and was undone, and it is the most credible three pages in the document. In an appendix it reads as a footnote; in the body it would carry the honesty argument that §8.2 currently carries by assertion.
