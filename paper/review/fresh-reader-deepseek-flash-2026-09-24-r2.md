## end-of-paper

## Whole-paper retell

An average reader in this field takes away the following. The paper argues that agent continuity has a third and deepest layer, "epistemic identity" — a self maintained as "calibrated, provenance-bearing, self-correcting knowledge" — that memory systems and narrative-self systems both miss, and it presents a running system, ENGRAM, that realizes it: typed claims with provenance, confidence computed from declared argument shape, a retraction cascade that marks every live claim-bearing dependent without being asked, an auto-surfacing hook that puts marked claims back in front of the agent, and a consolidation-time sweep that drives every marked claim to a decision. The argument for the layer is "convergence": philosophy of personal identity, cognitive science of memory, and belief-revision computer science each arrived at "identity as a persistent, inspectable, self-correcting record," and ENGRAM's designers arrived there too without having read them. On top of that convergence the paper builds its own claim, "identity as unique history," dropping Nozick's uniqueness requirement and filling his blank "closeness" metric with a retrieval profile. The evidence is one five-month graph, reported as *n* = 1 with the co-design confound named, plus a bounded eleven-source survey in which no other system satisfies Kahl's Condition 3. The paper's own read-level signature is a discipline of stated scope — the difficulty is that it states each scope three to five times, defers key specifics into appendices and unbuilt studies, and only resolves who its three bylined actors are in the Acknowledgements.

## Ordering

**Move §8.4 ("What we have not shown") to sit with §1.5, at the end of Chapter 1.** This is the one place in the paper that lists, in one screen, every limitation it will concede (the *n* = 1 confound, the deferred twin study, the narrower connectivity claim, the weight-persistence gap, the unmeasured improvement claim, calibration, variance-not-bias, the ranking constants, the feeling-report scope). Everything it contains was already available in scattered form from §3.8, §6.0, §7.0, and §7.1–7.3, but a reader must hold it across six chapters and two appendix cites ("**Appendix C.5**", "**Appendix D.5**") to know what the paper is and is not claiming. Chapter 1 §1.5 already promises *"name what the paper does not establish"*; delivering the map there would make the rest of the read a filling-in rather than a running tally.

**Second, smaller move: the verbatim OCC conditions currently at Appendix E §E.6 belong at Chapter 2 §2.7.** §2.7 introduces the "three jointly necessary **conditions** and four **constraints**" and gives Condition 1 as *"(verbatim in Appendix E §E.6)"* while paraphrasing Conditions 2 and 3.
